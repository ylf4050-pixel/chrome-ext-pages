Privacy Policy

Geo Simplify Chrome Extension · Last updated: 2026-07-31

Geo Simplify (“the Extension”) helps you simplify and clean geospatial files locally. This policy explains what data we access and what we do not do.

Data we access

When you choose or paste geo data, the Extension reads that content on your device to clean, preview, and export it. Export files are generated locally and downloaded to your computer.

Map tiles

The map preview loads raster tiles from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Your uploaded or pasted geo files are not uploaded to OpenStreetMap or to us; only standard tile image requests are made.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not operate a backend server for this Extension.
  • We do not upload your geo files, exports, or browsing history to our servers.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising trackers in the Extension.

Permissions

  • Host access to OpenStreetMap tile URLs — load map tiles for the preview only.

Local storage

The current version does not store personal information in cloud accounts.

Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the Extension changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly.
